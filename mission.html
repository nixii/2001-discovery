THE MISSION

Humanity's first manned mission to Jupiter

...and you can be a part of it.

Why?
Millions of years ago, humanity had a great leap forward. We invented the tool. With this newfound technology, we gained superiority over every other species on the Earth, and ultimately became a spacefaring civilization. We have just had our second greatest leap forwards; the ability to send people past the moon into deep space, to explore new worlds and advance us further.

About the Mission
Your destination is the gas giant Jupiter. You won't only be testing the limits of our technology, but will also discover anything that is around Jupiter that we were previously unable to explore. The results from your mission will pull humanity further ahead, and may bring upon another leap of advancement, transforming our species for the better.

The Discovery
Your ship, the Discovery, is the most advanced vehicle that has ever been built. Based on the tried-and-true space-faring technology of IBM, your ship will provide an efficient, safe, and comfortable trip to Jupiter. Unlike our old, dark spacecraft, the Discovery is light and is a safe haven from the vaccum of space. An advanced computer, HAL 9000, is on board, able to interact with the crew and handle all the functions of your spacecraft, so your job as a pilot will almost be like vacation.
