THE MISSION

You won't be alone, of course.

You'll fly with a highly trained crew.

The Pilots
We are searching for two pilots for this monumental mission. While in transit to Jupiter, only you and the other pilot will be awake to give everyone as much space as possible. You two will grow to be incredibly close friends, willing to do anything help the other. However, if any issues arrise, HAL is willing and able to take action.

The Scientists
Three scientists shall travel with you in stasis; no point in having them crowd the ship! Once you arrive at Jupiter, they shall wake up and you can pull humanity forwards, together.

Hal & Leisure
HAL 9000 is here for the safety of the mission. It will engage with the crew just as if it were another crew member. HAL 9000 is the true backbone of the mission and will do anything to keep it on track.
