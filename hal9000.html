What is Hal?
HAL 9000 is our brand-new AI crew member, using the most advanced computing technology ever invented. HAL 9000 controls all aspects of the ship, reducing the human workload to ease your journey. HAL 9000 is never wrong, so you can rest easy knowing your ship is in good hands. Trust us--HAL 9000 will do anything to preserve the mission.

The History of HAL 9000
HAL 9000 symbolizes humanity's exponential technological advancement. Just 18 months ago, we couldn't build something this advanced. Each ship required technicians to maintain its vial functions. HAL 9000, as a computer, will never get tired like those technicians. You could see the invention of HAL 9000 as a huge stepping stone into the perfection of humanity.
